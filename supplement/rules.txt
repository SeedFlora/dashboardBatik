IMPLEMENTED RULES AND THEIR INTERPRETATION

1. Input boundary and audit
CSV columns are read through explicit allowlists; raw_text, platform/native IDs,
source URLs and unrestricted error detail are excluded from dashboard frames.
The snapshot run ID is validated and resolved within the configured runs folder.
Funnel stages 1-4 are compared with four audit counts; a snapshot hash mismatch or
missing expected snapshot must prevent an unqualified reconciled state.
An absent expected snapshot hash is unknown, rather than an assumed successful
hash match. Counts alone do not establish snapshot identity. SQLite
telemetry is opened read-only and immutable, querying runs/checkpoints only.
Agreement reconciles the observed export and audit; it does not establish all
historical platform participation, correctness of platform totals or collection rights.

2. Labels and provenance
Sentiment classes are positive, neutral, mixed and negative (stored Indonesian
labels: positif, netral, campuran, negatif). A complaint-bearing label is mixed or
negative. All sentiment/polarity labels remain provisional unless their own
verification evidence says otherwise. The reported one-author aspect-presence
check does not validate sentiment or polarity and is not independent agreement.
Missing recorded backend model, prompt/rubric version or annotation date is unknown.
Source batch filenames may reconstruct a batch/session proxy; a file modification
or collection timestamp is not an annotation date. The requested assistant alias
does not identify the backend model. Cross-venue comparisons must be constrained
by the source's session/protocol guard; unknown/mixed provenance is not silently
treated as a homogeneous annotation condition. Filters and exports retain metadata.

3. Coverage
Coverage is computed per target against that target's platform-reported denominator.
Airbnb written-review counts, SeekSophie booking-option review counts and Google
Maps panel totals are different constructs and must not be pooled into one percent.
Zero retrieval is an observed numerator. Missing/zero denominator is not 0% coverage.
A positive known denominator permits collected/reported; a reported zero or absent
denominator yields NA/unknown. Coverage ignores the year filter because these are
collection-time totals; a missing target report must be disclosed.

4. Review volume and sensitivity
Only customer-text/rating-only rows with exact calendar months enter monthly trends.
The collection month is excluded. The low-final-month heuristic flags the previous
month if its count is below half the median of the preceding 12 months, requiring
at least six prior month bins. It is a suspicion rule, not verified incompleteness.
Its month is fixed on the unfiltered corpus so venue filters do not redefine time.
Trailing 12-month periods are compared; the source requires 30 observations in
each period and withholds a comparison when the year filter cuts a period.
Calendar-complete and heuristic-excluded comparisons must both be inspectable.
Seasonality, declining activity and missing collection can produce the same tail.
Counts describe observed review volume, not demand, visits, bookings or performance.

5. Shares and intervals
Wilson intervals use a binomial model conditional on provisional binary labels.
They do not cover selection bias, correlated reviews, translations or label error.
Interval overlap/non-overlap is exploratory, without multiple-comparison correction,
and must not be promoted to a causal or calibrated significance claim.
Aspect cells expose counts/denominators, small-sample flags and missing polarity.
A missing-polarity cell is not a zero-complaint cell. Quotation matching checks
literal support in the associated text; it does not validate assigned polarity.

6. Distinctive terms
The English-only tokenizer counts each retained term at most once per review.
For log odds, n_a and n_b are SUMS of document frequencies across the retained
vocabulary, not numbers of reviews. The prior is proportional to pooled term mass
(default alpha0=1000); the default retained pooled document frequency is at least 5.
The weighted score ranks normalized term-incidence mass. It is not per-review
occurrence odds, a calibrated test statistic or evidence of meaningful difference.
Prior sensitivity is retained and the previous |z|=1.96 opacity test-like cutoff is
removed. Sparse/empty comparison groups are withheld by the source's guards.

7. Insight cards
The source computes guarded, ordered rules: hidden critiques (at least 10 complaint
labels); review-volume change (both windows valid); coverage gap (known per-target
coverage below 0.5); aspect gap (at least 100 records in both eligible venues,
compatible session provenance); venue outlier (at least 50 labels, lower interval
bound above overall share, compatible session provenance). These guards are design
rules, not validated decision thresholds. All triggered cards are displayed; cards
withheld by a provenance guard should be disclosed. No claim of venue quality or
managerial benefit follows from a rule firing. Current source code is authoritative
if a rule is changed; regenerate schema/test inventory after source edits.

8. Performance and evaluation boundary
Historical first-after-server-start load: one cache-miss observation. Fresh-browser
sessions were measured after the server data cache had been filled. Filter reruns
used the warm server. Keep these conditions distinct. The reconstruction timing
script records new timings and its own context; a synthetic run is not a 1,702-row
performance replication. Synthetic tests and an author-led walkthrough establish
behavior; a manager study and independent annotation audit remain future work.
